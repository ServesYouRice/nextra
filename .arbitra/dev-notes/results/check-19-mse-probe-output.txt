Reading: Chromium's recorder ends every timeslice one byte into the next element (libwebm writes the
element ID byte by byte and the slice is cut on that write). When the old generation's queued chunks are
flushed, the SourceBuffer keeps the dangling bytes and takes all of the new generation (13 appends) without
buffering any of it: no error event, MediaSource still open, no video error, picture frozen. An MP4
SourceBuffer given the VP8 generation does the same. With abort() (+ changeType()) playback continues.
Firefox's recorder cuts at element boundaries, so Firefox-recorded VP8 survives the flush.

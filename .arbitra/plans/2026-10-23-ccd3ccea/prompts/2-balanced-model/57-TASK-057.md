<goal>
Guarantee that the process always terminates: non-zero after a fatal error during shutdown, zero after a clean graceful shutdown.

This matters because it fixes:
- Fatal-error handling becomes a no-op once a graceful shutdown has started, leaving the process half torn down (medium)
  handleUnexpectedProcessError delegates to requestFatalShutdown and returns immediately. requestFatalShutdown resolves without doing anything when isShuttingDown is already true. The graceful shutdown() later in server.js sets the same flag before it awaits, runs cleanupGlobalResources and arms its 5-second exit timer. If shutdown() rejects, or an uncaught error occurs while it is in progress, handleUnexpectedProcessError neither cleans up nor exits. That covers: - the explicit `.catch` for the SMOKE_TEST path; - an unhandled rejection from the SIGINT/SIGTERM handlers. If the throw happened before the exit timer was armed, nothing ever exits the process. The installed signal handlers also suppress default termination.
</goal>

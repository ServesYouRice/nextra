<goal>
Make OggOpusMuxer.pushRtp compute timestamp deltas from the last accepted packet instead of the first. Long streams (≈12.4 h at 48 kHz, or sooner with a large initial timestamp) must not permanently drop audio.

This matters because it fixes:
- Cumulative RTP timestamp delta exceeds signed 32-bit limit and permanently drops audio (high)
  OggOpusMuxer computes timestampDelta relative to the initial stream baseTimestamp and drops packets when timestampDelta exceeds 0x7fffffff. Because this.baseTimestamp is never updated, running for 2^31 samples (approx 12.4 hours at 48kHz, or sooner if initial timestamp was large) causes all subsequent packets to be permanently dropped.
</goal>

<context>

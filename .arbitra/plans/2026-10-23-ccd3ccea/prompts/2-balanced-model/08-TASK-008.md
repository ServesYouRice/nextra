# TASK-008 · Keep the declared GOP burst allowance until the burst is written and drained

<goal>
Stop writeVideoFrame from discarding the beginBurst() allowance on the first frame, so a replayed cached GOP is not dropped and does not trigger a spurious 'video-gap'.

This matters because it fixes:
- Immediate reset of _stdinCap in writeVideoFrame defeats burst buffer expansion (high)
  When beginBurst() is invoked to replay a cached GOP burst into FFmpeg, _stdinCap is raised to accommodate the queued frames. However, in writeVideoFrame(), the condition checking if writableLength is below or equal to MAX_STDIN_BUFFERED_BYTES executes on the very first frame before the burst has filled the stdin buffer. This immediately resets _stdinCap back to MAX_STDIN_BUFFERED_BYTES, causing subsequent frames in the burst to exceed the cap, get dropped, and trigger an unwanted video-gap event.
- The first video write clears the declared burst allowance (medium)
  <quotes> this._stdinCap = MAX_STDIN_BUFFERED_BYTES + Math.max(0, bytes) * 2; </quotes> beginBurst() increases the input allowance, but writeVideoFrame() immediately restores the ordinary cap whenever writableLength is at or below that cap. A fresh process normally satisfies that condition on its first write, so the declared allowance is discarded before the burst can build its queue.
</goal>

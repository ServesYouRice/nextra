<claim>
SPS/PPS are injected before every IDR slice, splitting multi-slice keyframes into separate access units (medium)

push() injects cached SPS/PPS before every NAL of type 5, not once per access unit. Some keyframes are coded as several slices, for example x264 with tune=zerolatency (which enables sliced threads, and this app allows that tune) and several hardware encoders. The output then becomes SPS PPS slice0 SPS PPS slice1 and so on. Under H.264 access-unit rules, an SPS or PPS that follows a VCL NAL starts a new access unit, and FFmpeg's h264 parser splits frames on exactly that. One keyframe in the raw Annex-B stream is therefore split into several pictures, each missing its leading slices. onKeyframe also fires once per slice, so cadence diagnostics over-count by the slice count.
</claim>

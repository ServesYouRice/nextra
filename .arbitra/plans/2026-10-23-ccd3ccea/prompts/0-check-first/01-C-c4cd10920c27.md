<claim>
Duplicate CAXACAXACAXA delimiter appended to Windows stub corrupts self-extracting executable (critical)

In prepareWindowsCaxaStub(), fs.appendFileSync explicitly appends '\nCAXACAXACAXA\n' to the prepared iconStub binary. However, the caxa packager itself automatically appends '\nCAXACAXACAXA\n' to separate the stub from the payload archive. Appending it beforehand creates two consecutive delimiter markers in the generated executable. At runtime, the caxa stub locates the first marker and expects the archive stream immediately after it, encountering the second marker string instead of valid archive header data, leading to decompression failure and immediate termination.
</claim>

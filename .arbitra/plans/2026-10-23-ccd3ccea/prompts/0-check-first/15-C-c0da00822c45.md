Expanded OBS card is capped at max-height 46rem and clipped by clip-path, so rows can be cut off at phone widths (medium)

When that happens the box is capped, and the overflowing rows are visually clipped and removed from hit-testing by clip-path. No overflow scrolling is defined in this window, so users have no way to reach those rows.

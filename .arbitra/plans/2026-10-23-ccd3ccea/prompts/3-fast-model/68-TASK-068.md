Prevent the HostView module from failing to evaluate when reading window.localStorage throws a SecurityError.

- Module-level window.localStorage access is not guarded and can make the HostView module fail to load (medium)

In Chromium, reading that property throws a SecurityError when site data is blocked. Examples are a 'block all cookies' setting or a sandboxed iframe without storage access.
